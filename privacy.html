Privacy Policy

Prompt Mommy (Instagram @promptmommy) · Last updated 5 November 2026

In short: our app only reads pins from Pinterest boards that we own. It does not access other people's accounts, does not post or change anything on Pinterest, and does not sell or share any data.

Who we are

Prompt Mommy is an independent content creator that publishes AI image prompt guides on Instagram as @promptmommy. This policy covers the Pinterest app we use internally (the "App").

What the App does

The App connects to the Pinterest API using our own Pinterest account. It reads the pins saved on our own boards so we can study general visual style, such as colour palette, pose and camera angle, as private reference when planning original content. We create our own images and do not republish pins or images from Pinterest.

Information we access

With the read-only permissions boards:read, pins:read and user_accounts:read, the App accesses:

  • basic details of our own Pinterest account (username and account ID);
  • the names of our own boards;
  • pins on those boards: image, title, description and link.

The App does not ask other Pinterest users to log in and does not collect any information about them. It never writes, posts, edits or deletes anything on Pinterest.

How we store and use it

  • Pin data is kept in private storage that only we can access.
  • Reference images are deleted automatically within 7 days.
  • We use the data only to plan our own original content.
  • Access tokens and app keys are kept in secure environment storage, never in code or public files.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Pinterest data with anyone. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or tracking.

Your choices

Because the App only reads our own boards, it holds no data about you. If you believe any of your information has reached us through the App, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes

If the App changes what it accesses, we will update this page and the date at the top.
